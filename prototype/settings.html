Risk levels

Fixed definition (FR-08), shown for reference

Level Rule Items (run #12)
HighProjected stock reaches the safety stock within the item's lead time4
MediumProjected stock reaches the safety stock within the forecast horizon, but after the lead time7
LowProjected stock stays above the safety stock for the whole horizon19

Projected stock of a week = current stock + planned receipts due by that week − cumulative forecast up to that week. Expected stock-out date = the first week in which the projected stock is at or below the safety stock.

Effect of a change is shown as the new counts by level before the pharmacist confirms (planned for the Vue implementation).
